# Beginner Earth Observation Analysis Series 2026

**24–25 September 2026 · Lund, Sweden**

Teaching materials and practical exercises from the **SESAC Beginner Earth Observation Analysis Series**, a beginner-friendly introduction to Earth Observation, GIS and spatial analysis.

The training was designed for participants with little or no previous experience in Earth Observation, GIS or programming.

## About the course

The two-day programme introduced participants to the foundations of Earth Observation and demonstrated how satellite and geospatial information can be connected to societal questions.

Topics included:

- Earth Observation fundamentals
- Satellite platforms, sensors and imagery
- Spatial, temporal and spectral resolution
- Open and commercial Earth Observation data
- GIS and spatial data
- Visual interpretation of satellite imagery
- Urban and sustainable-development applications
- Flood mapping
- Critical-infrastructure accessibility
- Interpretation, context and limitations of Earth Observation

The course combined lectures, demonstrations and guided practical exercises.

## Programme and materials

### Day 1 — 24 September

#### Earth Observation, Social Science and SESAC

**Ola Hall**

Introduction to Earth Observation, SESAC and the opportunities and limitations of connecting satellite-derived information with social-science research.

#### Earth Observation Fundamentals: From Satellites to Spatial Data

**Stefanos Georganos**

A beginner-friendly introduction to satellite systems, sensors, imagery, resolution, data availability and the connection between Earth Observation and GIS.

📂 [Session materials](day-1/02-earth-observation-fundamentals/)

#### Contextualising Earth Observation Data through Visual Interpretation

**Lina Eklund**

An introduction to contextual interpretation of satellite imagery and the role of local knowledge, visual interpretation and landscape context in Earth Observation analysis.

The public material for this session consists of the **Google Earth Pro practical exercise**. The lecture presentation is not included in the public repository because it contains imagery that is not cleared for public redistribution.

📂 [Practical exercise and documentation](day-1/03-visual-interpretation/)

---

### Day 2 — 25 September

#### Earth Observation for Urban and Sustainable Development Applications

**Stefanos Georganos**

Examples of how Earth Observation and GIS can support analysis of urban growth, the built environment, environmental exposure, inequality, sustainability and climate-related risks.

📂 [Session materials](day-2/01-urban-sustainable-development/)

#### Hands-on Workshop: From Flood Mapping to Critical Infrastructure Accessibility

**Stefanos Georganos & Amalia Chantziara**

A guided practical workflow connecting Earth Observation-based flood information with roads, critical infrastructure and accessibility analysis.

The exercise follows the analytical chain:

**Earth Observation and flood information → spatial data integration → affected infrastructure → accessibility analysis → interpretation**

No previous programming experience is required.

The workshop materials include the presentation, a Google Colab/Jupyter notebook, supporting raster datasets, detailed instructions and licensing information.

📂 [Workshop materials and instructions](day-2/02-flood-accessibility-workshop/)

## Repository contents

The repository provides the publicly shareable materials from the two-day training, including:

- Selected lecture slides

- A Google Colab/Jupyter notebook for the flood-accessibility workshop

Some materials used during the training are not included in this public repository where redistribution permissions do not allow public sharing.
