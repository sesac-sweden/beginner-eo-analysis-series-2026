# Earth Observation for Urban and Sustainable Development Applications

**Instructor:** Stefanos Georganos  
**25 September 2026 · SESAC Beginner Earth Observation Analysis Series**

This session provides an overview of how Earth Observation and GIS can be used to study cities and support sustainable-development monitoring.

Examples cover themes such as:

- Urban growth and land-use change
- Urban form and the built environment
- Environmental exposure and vulnerability
- Urban inequalities
- Sustainable Development Goal monitoring
- Disaster and climate-related risks

The emphasis is on connecting satellite observations and spatial analysis with societal questions rather than on technical implementation.
